FAQ

Questions

What happens when I tap the handle?

Android's own volume bar opens, exactly like pressing a hardware volume key. Use its slider as normal.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

Android only lets an app draw a touch handle above other apps through an accessibility service. Gesture Volume does not read your screen, keystrokes or messages.

Does it work if my buttons are fully broken?

Yes, that is the whole point. It talks to Android's audio system directly, so no physical button is involved.

Can I change how long the handle stays active?

Yes. Pick 2, 3, 5 or 10 seconds in Settings. Try it in the demo above.

How does the double-tap screenshot work?

Double-tap the handle and Gesture Volume briefly hides it, then asks Android to take a screenshot (Android 9 or newer). You can turn it off in Settings.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, works offline and has no ads inside it. This website shows ads to help cover costs. The source is viewable on GitHub under its own license.
