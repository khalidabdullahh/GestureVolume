How Gesture Volume Works

Three natural steps. No switching apps, no fumbling with worn physical buttons.

1

Touch Edge Handle

A subtle frosted pill rests quietly on your chosen screen edge (customizable side and vertical height to avoid keyboards).

2

Swipe Up / Down (↕)

Slide finger upward to raise volume, downward to lower. The sleek on-screen HUD updates smoothly in real time.

3

Auto Disappears (✨)

Lift your finger and continue what you were doing. The volume HUD seamlessly auto-dismisses after 3 seconds of inactivity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the app need Accessibility Service permission?

Android requires Accessibility Services to display the subtle edge gesture handle and temporary volume HUD overlay when other apps are active. Gesture Volume uses this strictly for the on-screen volume handle and never inspects or collects any private user text or screen content.

Does it work if my phone volume buttons are completely broken?

Yes! That is the core purpose of Gesture Volume. It connects directly to Android's internal AudioManager API to change volume without needing any physical buttons.

Does Gesture Volume drain the battery?

No. When you are not touching the handle, the app enters an event-driven idle state with zero background loops and zero wake-locks. It also immediately resets when the screen turns off.
