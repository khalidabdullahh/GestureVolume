How Gesture Volume Works

Three natural steps. No need to switch apps or search for hardware buttons.

1

Draw Circle (⭕)

Draw a circular motion anywhere on your phone screen. Works on top of any game, video player, social media, or browser.

2

Swipe Up / Down (↕)

Slide finger upward to increase volume, or downward to lower it. The modern volume HUD responds in real-time with tactile clicks.

3

Auto Disappears (✨)

Lift your finger and continue your task. After 3 seconds of inactivity, the volume HUD smoothly fades away into zero memory usage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the app need Accessibility Service permission?

Android requires Accessibility Services to detect touch gestures globally when other applications are active on the screen and to display the temporary volume HUD overlay. Gesture Volume uses this exclusively for the circle gesture and does not inspect or collect any user data.

Does it work if my phone volume buttons are completely broken?

Yes! That is the core purpose of Gesture Volume. It connects directly to Android's internal AudioManager API to change volume without needing any physical buttons.

Does Gesture Volume drain the battery?

No. When you are not drawing gestures, the app enters an event-driven idle state with zero background loops and zero wake-locks. It also immediately resets when the screen turns off.
